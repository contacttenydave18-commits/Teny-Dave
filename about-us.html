Meet the Founder

A Passion for Books and the Stories Behind Them

I founded Teny Dave Book Marketing with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between talented authors and the readers who are searching for their books. Every author invests countless hours, energy, and piece of their soul into writing a manuscript, but without strategic promotional support, even extraordinary books can struggle to find their audience.

"Marketing is not about forcing a book onto readers — it is about illuminating what makes a story special so the right readers can discover it naturally."

My background combines strategic marketing expertise with a deep appreciation for literature across genres. Over the years, I have helped independent writers, self-published authors, and debut storytellers navigate the complexities of book promotion, transform their ideas into cohesive campaigns, and build author platforms that stand the test of time.

I believe that author marketing should be as thoughtful, nuanced, and authentic as the writing itself. That is why I work directly with each client, taking the time to understand their unique voice, audience, and definition of publishing success.

How We Work

A Thoughtful, Author-Centered Approach

We break down the complex world of book marketing into a structured, stress-free process designed around your specific project and goals.

01

Discovery & Understanding

We start by getting to know you, your manuscript, your target readers, and your ultimate ambitions for your publishing career. No templates or automated solutions — just genuine listening.

02

Research & Strategy Development

We analyze your genre's landscape, identify key reader communities, and outline a realistic, actionable marketing roadmap designed specifically for your book.

03

Creative Content & Brand Crafting

We design promotional assets, write engaging copy, refine your messaging, and construct an author presence that authentically reflects your creative voice.

04

Campaign Execution & Delivery

05

Review & Platform Growth
